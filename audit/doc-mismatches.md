# Where the documentation and the code disagree

[Back to the summary](README.md)

Two readers went claim by claim through README.md and CLAUDE.md, and through `docs/*.md`
and TODO.md. Every other reader also reported mismatches with docstrings and comments in
its own area. A claim counts here when the code does not do what it says, does it
differently, or cannot be found. 97 findings are pure documentation mismatches.
184 more are real defects that *also* contradict a document (second table).

## The ones that change what someone would do

These are claims a person or an agent would act on, where acting on them goes wrong.
